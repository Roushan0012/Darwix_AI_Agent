# Frequently Asked Questions & Common Objections

## Objection 1: "Why is your interest rate higher than traditional tier-1 commercial banks?"
**Response & Grounded Rationale**:
Traditional commercial banks typically advertise nominal rates of 7% - 10% APR, but their qualification process requires physical real estate collateral, audited financial statements for 3+ years, and approval turnaround cycles of 60 to 90 days with high rejection rates (>75% for small businesses).
Darwix Capital provides **unsecured financing** (no real estate collateral required), automated underwriting based on real-time cash flow and bank statements, and funds disbursed directly to your operating account within **24 to 48 hours**. We solve urgent liquidity needs where timing is critical.

## Objection 2: "Do I need to pledge my home or commercial property as collateral?"
**Response & Grounded Rationale**:
No. Our Working Capital Express and SME Growth facilities are **100% unsecured** regarding physical assets. We do not place liens on your personal residential property. We require only a standard corporate resolution and a principal director's personal guarantee to ensure business commitment.

## Objection 3: "Are there hidden fees or surprise charges?"
**Response & Grounded Rationale**:
Darwix Capital operates on complete fee transparency. There are only two fee components:
1. The agreed nominal interest rate stated clearly in your term sheet.
2. A one-time origination processing fee (between 2.5% and 3.0%) which is deducted directly at disbursement.
There are zero application fees, zero monthly maintenance fees, and zero prepayment penalties if the loan is paid off after 6 months.

## FAQ 4: "What happens if my business revenue temporarily dips?"
**Response & Grounded Rationale**:
Borrowers in good standing who face temporary cash flow seasonality may request a payment relief restructuring or a temporary tenor extension by notifying their account manager at least 10 business days prior to the next billing cycle.
