# Section 1: Minimum Borrower Qualification Criteria

To qualify for an unsecured SME Working Capital Loan or Growth Facility, the applicant business must meet the following mandatory baseline thresholds:

1. **Operating History**: The business entity must have been registered and actively operating for a minimum of 12 consecutive months. Startups under 12 months require a secured asset-backed pledge or a creditworthy corporate guarantor.
2. **Revenue Requirements**: Minimum verified average monthly revenue of $15,000 over the preceding 6 months (supported by official business bank statements or VAT filings).
3. **Credit Profile**: Principal director or guarantor must possess a minimum credit bureau score of 620 with no active bankruptcies, court judgments, or defaults in the trailing 24 months.
4. **Geography & Registration**: Must be a legally registered business entity (LLC, Corporation, or Registered Sole Proprietorship) operating within approved jurisdictions.

# Section 2: Loan Amounts, Tenors, and Pricing Structure

- **Working Capital Express**:
  - Facility Size: $10,000 to $100,000
  - Tenor: 3 to 18 months
  - Repayment Frequency: Weekly or Monthly ACH / direct debit
  - Nominal Interest Rate: 1.25% - 2.10% per month (effective APR 15% - 25.2%)
  - Origination Fee: 3.0% deducted at disbursement

- **SME Growth Expansion Loan**:
  - Facility Size: $100,000 to $500,000
  - Tenor: 12 to 36 months
  - Repayment Frequency: Monthly amortized
  - Nominal Interest Rate: 0.95% - 1.50% per month
  - Origination Fee: 2.5% deducted at disbursement

# Section 3: Early Repayment & Pre-termination Policy

Borrowers may repay their loan in full at any time prior to the maturity date.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: Zero penalty for early repayment after 6 months of on-time payments.
- If prepayment occurs within the first 6 months, a flat administrative rebate adjustment fee of 1.5% of the remaining principal balance applies.
- Interest is charged only on the pro-rated period during which capital was deployed.
